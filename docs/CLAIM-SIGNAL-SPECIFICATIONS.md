## Prior-day breakout rail clarification

There is no Dual Thrust card in the A6 22-card DRAFT set. The deleted A5 Dual Thrust-like implementation used prior-day high/low bounds with k=0; the opened sources did not establish universal textbook defaults. Among the surviving prior-day breakout rules, `pdh-pdl-break` compares current high/low against the previous EAT trading day's observed high/low. Neither rail is measured from the prior day's open. The separate PDH/PDL retest cards use prior-day high or low as levels, not an open-anchored rail.
