**Status:** DRAFT; not frozen, not strategy-tested. Generated from signal-params.mjs, lib/signals.mjs, and claim cards.
**Common:** input bars are M30, EAT chronological order. Each evaluator emits a directional signal and attaches the Map verdict-state label; claim cells later partition those emissions into EFFICIENT/CHOPPY. All indicators are causal (no future bars). Evaluator uses bar high/low intrabar for range breaks and close crossings as stated below. No trade outcome, exit, cost or P&L is produced.

Several entryAt=close fields are metadata only: channel/range evaluators actually trigger from current high/low. This dump records actual current DRAFT code rather than silently correcting a card.

## Dual Thrust textbook source (opened; parameter status)

- **VERIFIED, opened 2026-10-28:** FMZ Quant, “Implement a Dual Thrust Digital Currency Quantitative Trading Strategy in Python,” https://www.fmz.com/digest-topic/9872. It states Range=max(HH-LC,HC-LL), threshold lines open + K1*Range and open - K2*Range, says K1/K2 are adjustable, and its demonstration uses K1=K2=0.5. This is an opened implementation article, not a universal canonical parameter prescription.
- **VERIFIED, opened 2026-10-28:** ProRealCode, “Dual Thrust Strategy indicator,” https://www.prorealcode.com/prorealtime-indicators/dual-thrust-strategy-indicator/. It independently states the HH/LC/HC/LL range formula and K1/K2 lines, but does not specify one fixed N/K pair in the article body.

Do not interpret its present signal-count Jaccard as a test of canonical Dual Thrust.
