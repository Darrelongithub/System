## 1. Why this pass

v1.7 fixed `src/lib/analyzer/status.ts#parseTime` so offset-less candle timestamps are always interpreted as EAT (`+03:00`), whether the date/time separator is a space or a `T` (B1 in `logs/v1.7-changes.md`).

That fix shipped **without a dedicated regression test**. The golden fixture cannot catch the bug (all golden candles are space-separated), and on a Nairobi host (`Africa/Nairobi` = UTC+3) the pre-fix body happened to produce the same answer as the fixed body — so a developer machine in Nairobi could never observe the failure.

This pass closes that gap.

## 3. What the tests assert (and why they bite)

### 3.1 Mixed separators (`status-parse-time-tz`)

Pre-v1.7 body:

```ts
const ms = Date.parse(value.includes("T") ? value : value.replace(" ", "T") + "+03:00");
```

On a UTC host, a `T`-separated candle is host-local (UTC) while a space-separated candle is forced to EAT. After a space-separated fill at `11:30`, a `T`-separated `12:00` candle is read as three hours later than wall clock, so the Crabel ORB one-hour breakeven clock sees ~180 min elapsed after 30 real minutes and moves the stop to entry three candles early.

The synthetic scenario dips every post-fill candle to 99 (between entry 100 and SL 90). If breakeven is wrongly active, 99 ≤ 100 resolves on the +30 min candle (index 2). Correct behaviour resolves on the +60 min candle (index 3) with `statusNote` matching `/breakeven stop hit/`.

### 3.2 Host-TZ matrix (`tz-matrix`)

Re-running the suite under four host TZs is what would have caught the pre-v1.7 bug on a Nairobi developer machine. Against the fixed `parseTime` every TZ passes; against the pre-v1.7 body the matrix fails under `TZ=UTC` and `TZ=America/New_York` with *"breakeven must activate at +60 min… got candle 2"*, and passes under `Africa/Nairobi` (the accidental local-offset match).
