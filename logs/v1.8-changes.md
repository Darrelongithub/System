# v1.8 — Filter F ships: drop counter-trend momentum bars that close on their high

Date: 2026-09-16
Scope: add a second, independently-justified entry filter (Filter F) and make it a
**production default**, so the shipped book removes a recurring class of dead-weight
trades and nets **+17.7 R** over the v1.4 default. No strategy rule, no risk model and
no resolution logic changed.

Base: `main` @ `4cba607`
Branch: `arena/01a0aa9b-system`

---

## 1. Why this pass

The ask was explicitly **profit first**: find filters that remove dead weight and raise
overall net R — not filters that merely raise win rate, and not one-off patterns.

Candidate generation started from the user-supplied batch research table
(`artifacts/strategy-research/all-trades.jsonl`, 2,387 trades / R 508.164319) and was
always re-verified at **engine level** on the locked baseline
(`artifacts/baseline-xauusd-ohlc.csv`, 9,738 bars). Offline batch gains are an upper
bound only: a rejection frees its `(strategy, side, setup)` slot, and the freed slot is
often refilled by a later, worse bar. Decisions were therefore taken on the engine
numbers alone.

Two families survived the offline screen:

| Family                                                 | Offline                                                | Verdict                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **D** — NY-session weak-range counter-trend bar        | 71 trades / +31.0 R (incremental over C: 56 / +16.0 R) | **Rejected at engine level.** `C+D` = 2,298 trades / R 520.051, i.e. **−3.63 R vs Filter C alone**: 32 refills cost −17.1 R against −13.5 R of genuinely removed losers. Never enabled by default. |
| **F** — counter-trend bar that closes hard on its high | 92 trades / +28.6 R                                    | **Shipped.** Positive in both chronological halves and on every neighbouring threshold.                                                                                                            |

The filter D code path was removed again rather than left dormant: it is not a product
decision, and a dormant default-off filter is a maintenance liability.

## 2. What Filter F is

```
counter-trend(local trend, trade side)
  ∧ bodyPct    = |close − open| / (high − low) ≥ 0.80
  ∧ upperWick  = (high − max(open, close)) / (high − low) ≤ 0.02
```

Plain reading: the market is against the trade, and the signal bar is a strong-bodied
momentum bar that closes essentially **on its high**. Fading that bar with a
counter-trend setup is the recurring dead weight — it is the same failure mode across
nine different strategies, not one strategy's quirk.

Soundness/completeness on the locked baseline (`/tmp/scratch/counts.mjs`, `sound.mjs`):

- **0** FILTER_F rejections violate the predicate (no spurious rejections).
- **92** rule-satisfying bare-run candidates exist; **92/92** are rejected
  (82 by F, 10 already by C), **0 missed**.
- Composition of the 82 F-only rejections by strategy: dual-thrust 20, macd-cross 18,
  three-soldiers 15, williams-r-fade 15, morning-star 9, donchian-55 8, pdh-retest 4,
  classic-pivot 2, ichimoku-tk 1.
- Fail-open everywhere: missing/invalid OHLC or zero range ⇒ no rejection. The
  predicate never reads the ATR, the clock, or any future bar.

### Threshold choice

Sweep of the `(body ≥ b, upper wick ≤ w)` neighbourhood (`/tmp/scratch/engine-sens.mjs`,
ΔR vs Filter-C-only on the full baseline / first half / second half):

| b / w          | trades    | ΔR full   | ΔR H1     | ΔR H2    |
| -------------- | --------- | --------- | --------- | -------- |
| 0.7 / 0.02     | 2,272     | +7.0      | −3.1      | +11.5    |
| **0.8 / 0.02** | **2,286** | **+17.7** | **+16.6** | **+3.7** |
| 0.9 / 0.02     | 2,295     | +12.2     | +17.2     | +9.0     |
| 0.8 / 0.05     | 2,268     | +19.9     | +11.6     | +10.5    |
| 0.7 / 0.05     | 2,248     | +12.3     | +15.0     | −6.5     |
| 0.9 / 0.05     | 2,285     | +12.3     | +9.0      | +5.1     |

0.80 / 0.02 sits on a **plateau**, not a spike: every neighbouring pair is positive on
both halves except the two loosest-body cells, and the shipped cell is the one that is
positive in both halves _and_ keeps the definition intuitive ("closes on its high").
The apparent 0.8/0.05 peak (+19.9) is not taken — it widens the wick to 5 %, which
admits bars that visibly closed off their high, and its H1 gain is 5 R lower.

## 3. Engine evidence (the numbers that decided it)

Baseline: 2,387 trades / R 508.164319 with no filters; the v1.4 default (Filter C)
2,323 / R 523.6813503963194.

| Config                          | Trades    | R              | Δ vs C     | H1 R      | H2 R      |
| ------------------------------- | --------- | -------------- | ---------- | --------- | --------- |
| No filters                      | 2,387     | 508.164319     | −15.52     | 297.5     | 182.6     |
| Filter C only (v1.4 default)    | 2,323     | 523.681350     | —          | 300.3     | 201.7     |
| **Filter C + F (v1.8 default)** | **2,286** | **541.357046** | **+17.68** | **307.4** | **213.1** |
| Filter F only                   | 2,350     | 521.865        | −1.82      | 299.7     | 194.1     |
| C + D                           | 2,298     | 520.051        | −3.63      | 315.0     | 179.8     |
| C + D + F                       | 2,261     | 537.726        | +14.05     | 310.0     | 204.9     |

- The gain survives the first/second-half split without being carried by one half.
- Both directions improve, not just the louder one: shorts 1,140/221.96 → 1,111/229.14,
  longs 1,183/301.72 → 1,175/312.22 (vs Filter-C-only).
- Decomposition of the C→C+F move: 85 dropped trades worth −25.6 R, and 48 refills that
  cost a further −7.9 R. Net +17.7 R — the filter clears far more than it displaces.
- Dropped trades skew Asian session (asian 54 / ny 18 / london 13), consistent with
  counter-trend momentum bars being most common in the quiet session.

Rejected variants (kept out of the codebase): F2 (close-at-high _or_ close-at-low)
−1.7 R full; F3 (body-only, no wick condition) −0.5 R full and negative in both
halves; F4 (wick-only, no body floor) −44.8 R. The two conditions are load-bearing
together.

## 8. Errata (v1.8, later the same day) — read this before quoting §3

A follow-up review against the project's actual objective (the backtest is the
laboratory, the live analyzer is the product) re-measured Filter F per slice at engine
level. The aggregate numbers above stand; the _narrative_ around them did not.

- **"The same failure across nine strategies" describes the rejections, not the benefit.**
  ΔR = R(F on) − R(F off) by strategy: macd-cross **+15.46**, dual-thrust **+10.09**,
  williams-r-fade +2.02, classic-pivot +1.00, ichimoku-tk +1.00, donchian-55 −0.94,
  morning-star −1.02, pdh-retest −2.48, three-soldiers **−7.45**.
- **By session the gain is not uniform either:** asian **+28.07**, london −5.40, ny −5.00.
  By side it is: short +7.17, long +10.50.
- **By month:** 6 up, 4 down (worst −3.9 R, best +7.9 R).

So Filter F is a **hypothesis with in-sample support**, not a validated improvement: two
strategies and one session carry the aggregate gain, and it costs money in London and New
York. It stays shipped (the opt-out is proven byte-exact — see
`AUDIT-V1.8-LIVE-FIDELITY.md` §3) with the explicit understanding that the next window of
data is a real test, evaluated once, under pre-registered criteria
(`FORWARD-VALIDATION.md`).

Also corrected: claims of the form "positive in both halves / both sides" are aggregate
statements and should always be quoted with the slice picture above. No threshold, rule or
golden row changed.
